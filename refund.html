Refund Policy

At ZFB Pharmaceutical, we want every customer to have a reliable and satisfactory experience. This Refund Policy explains when a return, replacement, or refund may be available for eligible purchases.

Returns

Products may be eligible for return if they arrive damaged, defective, or different from the product originally ordered, subject to applicable laws and product-specific requirements.

Customers should contact our support team as soon as possible after receiving an incorrect or damaged order.

Refund Eligibility

A return or refund request may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • Damaged products received during delivery.
  • Incorrect products supplied with an order.
  • Eligible manufacturing defects.
  • Other circumstances required by applicable consumer protection laws.

Non-Returnable Products

For safety and regulatory reasons, certain pharmaceutical and healthcare products may not be eligible for return once they have been delivered or opened.

  • Opened medicines or pharmaceutical products.
  • Products that have been used or altered.
  • Expired products caused by improper customer storage.
  • Products damaged through misuse or improper handling.
  • Products that are otherwise restricted from return under applicable law.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or contains an incorrect product, please contact ZFB Pharmaceutical customer support with your order information and a description of the issue.

We may request photographs or other information necessary to assess the reported issue.

Refund Process

Once a refund request has been reviewed and approved, the refund will be processed using the applicable original payment method where possible.

Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

Replacement

Where appropriate and available, ZFB Pharmaceutical may offer a replacement product instead of a refund for an eligible damaged or incorrect item.

Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible. Once an order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Any applicable refund will be handled according to the circumstances of the order and applicable laws.

Refund Timing

Approved refunds are generally initiated within 5–10 business days, although the time required for the funds to appear in your account may depend on your payment provider or financial institution.
