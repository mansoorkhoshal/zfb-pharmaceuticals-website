Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ZFB Pharmaceutical collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our website, request a consultation, or place an order.

Introduction

At ZFB Pharmaceutical, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you browse our website, contact our team, request a consultation, or place an order.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Shipping and Delivery Address
  • Order Details
  • Customer Support Messages
  • Consultation Requests
  • Information you voluntarily provide through forms, WhatsApp, or Email

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information only for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Confirming order and delivery information.
  • Responding to customer support requests.
  • Responding to consultation inquiries.
  • Providing information about our products and services.
  • Improving our website and customer experience.
  • Protecting our website against fraudulent or unauthorized activity.

Order Information

When you place an order, we may collect information necessary to process and fulfill that order. This may include your name, contact information, delivery address, selected products, and other information required to complete the transaction.

WhatsApp and Email Communications

If you choose to contact ZFB Pharmaceutical through WhatsApp or Email, the information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry, consultation request, or order.

Please avoid sending highly sensitive medical or personal information through general messaging channels unless it is specifically requested by an appropriate healthcare professional.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, remember preferences, understand website usage, and provide a better browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of transmitting information over the internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing Your Information

ZFB Pharmaceutical does not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, process orders, deliver products, provide technical services, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use or link to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, social media platforms, payment services, analytics tools, or other external services.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing their policies when using their services.

Healthcare Information Disclaimer

Information available on the ZFB Pharmaceutical website is provided for general informational purposes and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions regarding medicines, supplements, medical conditions, or treatment.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws and regulations, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request information about how your data is handled.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including fulfilling orders, providing support, maintaining business records, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact our support team.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
