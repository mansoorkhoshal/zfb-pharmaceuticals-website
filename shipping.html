Shipping Policy

At ZFB Pharmaceutical, we aim to provide a reliable and convenient delivery experience. This Shipping Policy explains how orders are processed, shipped, tracked, and delivered.

Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after order confirmation.

Processing may take longer during public holidays, promotional periods, unusually high order volumes, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Delivery Time

Estimated delivery times may vary depending on the destination, shipping provider, product availability, customs procedures, and other circumstances.

  • Local Delivery: Approximately 2–5 business days.
  • International Delivery: Approximately 7–14 business days.

Delivery estimates are provided for guidance only and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on factors such as destination, order size, delivery method, and applicable shipping requirements.

Any applicable shipping charges will be communicated during the ordering or checkout process.

Order Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information will be provided after your order has been dispatched.

Customers can use the provided tracking information to monitor the progress of their shipment through the applicable delivery provider.

Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete delivery information when placing an order.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses may result in delivery delays, additional charges, or unsuccessful delivery.

Delivery Delays

While we work to meet estimated delivery times, delays may occur because of circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

  • Customs clearance procedures.
  • Severe weather conditions.
  • Public holidays.
  • Transportation or courier disruptions.
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery information.
  • Other unforeseen circumstances.

International Shipping

International shipments may be subject to customs requirements, import restrictions, duties, taxes, or other regulations imposed by the destination country.

Customers are responsible for complying with applicable import requirements and any charges that may be imposed by the destination country's authorities, where applicable.

Failed or Missed Delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed because the recipient is unavailable or the delivery information is incorrect, the courier may attempt redelivery or return the shipment according to its procedures.

Customers should contact ZFB Pharmaceutical support if they experience a delivery issue.

Damaged Packages

If your package appears damaged when delivered, please contact our support team as soon as reasonably possible and provide your order information and details of the issue.

Additional information or photographs may be requested to help us assess the situation.
